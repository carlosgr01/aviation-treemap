Flight Cancellations: Exploring the Causes

Explore reported domestic airline cancellations in India from 2021 to 2023. The sunburst groups cancellations by cause, year and airline; larger sectors represent more reported cancellations.

Author: Carlos Gracia Ruiz  ·  Programme: Master’s Degree in Data Science
Course: Data Visualization · PEC 2

How to read it

Start at the centre and click a segment to zoom into that branch. Use the centre label or the chart’s navigation controls to move back up the hierarchy.

  • Inner ring: cancellation cause.
  • Middle ring: year (2021–2023).
  • Outer ring: airline.
  • Area: reported cancellation count.

Scope and caveats: these are reported cancellation counts for scheduled domestic airlines in India, not a worldwide dataset. Airline coverage varies by year, and counts are absolute values rather than cancellation rates; comparisons do not account for the number of departures operated by each airline.
