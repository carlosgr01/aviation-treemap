How to read it: larger rectangles indicate more airports recorded in OurAirports. Hover over a country for its count and share; click a continent or country to zoom in, and use the breadcrumb at the top of the chart to move back up the hierarchy.

Data and method. Source: OurAirports open-data downloads (airports.csv and countries.csv). Counts include records classified as large_airport, medium_airport, or small_airport; heliports, seaplane bases, balloonports and closed-airport records are excluded. Country names are joined using the country-code table. The dataset is community-maintained and may contain omissions or errors; counts describe this database, not airport traffic, passenger volume or commercial importance. The CSVs are fetched from the public data mirror when the page opens. Last accessed: 25 October 2026.
